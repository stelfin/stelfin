Send stablecoins on Stellar,
right from WhatsApp.

stelfin is a non-custodial stablecoin wallet you talk to instead of open. No app to install, no seed phrase to lose track of — send money to a phone number the way you'd send a text.

Chat on WhatsApp →

How it works

  1. 1

    Message stelfin. "send 5,000 to my brother" — plain language, no forms.

  2. 2

    Confirm on your device. A link opens the exact payment for you to sign — amount, recipient, nothing hidden.

  3. 3

    It lands on Stellar. Settled on-chain in seconds, sponsored so you never need to hold XLM.

Why non-custodial

  • Your key lives on your device. stelfin cannot move your funds — every send is signed by you, not us.
  • Every payment is confirmed on a page that reads the transaction itself, not our word for it.
  • An LLM helps read your message, but it never decides an amount or an address — everything it claims is checked against your own words before anything is built to sign.
