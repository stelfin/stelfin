Security

Built non-custodial, on purpose.

A custodial wallet moving money for you is a business holding your funds. stelfin is software you hold the key to instead.

  • Your key never leaves your device

    stelfin cannot move your funds. Every send is signed by you — the server never holds a spending key, so there's nothing here to steal or subpoena.

  • Confirmed on a page that reads the transaction itself

    The confirmation screen parses the envelope you're about to sign, not our word for it. If our summary ever disagreed with the transaction, it would refuse to show a send button at all.

  • The model never decides an amount or address

    An LLM helps read your message, but every amount and destination it claims is checked against your own words before anything is built to sign. It extracts; it never authorizes.
